# Code of Conduct

Dot Lab is a learning and research project. We want everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews or any other project space) to feel welcome, whatever their experience level, background or identity.

This code is based on the principles of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many contributors are learning; questions are welcome.
- Give and receive feedback about the work, not the person. Explain the reasons behind a review comment.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding someone meant harm.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Respect people's time and privacy. Do not share anyone's personal information without their explicit permission.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or derogatory comments about anyone's identity or background.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention or advances.
- Threats, intimidation, or encouraging others to harass someone.
- Publishing others' private information (such as addresses, email addresses or messages) without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, or other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project in public (for example, posting as a maintainer).

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub: [@Anjub004](https://github.com/Anjub004). If the matter involves a security vulnerability, use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Please include links to the relevant issues, comments or messages. Reports are handled confidentially, and reporters will not face retaliation for reports made in good faith.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews every report and may take any action they consider appropriate, from most to least lenient:

1. **Correction**: a private message explaining what was inappropriate, possibly with a request for a public apology.
2. **Warning**: a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and a period without interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban**: no participation in project spaces for a set period.
4. **Permanent ban**: for a pattern of violations or a single serious one, such as harassment.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may be removed from the project.
